The companion animation frames (blink, look-board, look-mat, nod, hop-crouch,
hop-air, think, cheer and talk for each companion) were generated with Meshy
(https://www.meshy.ai) on its free plan, as image-to-image edits of each
companion's painted idle frame. Meshy licenses free-plan output under the
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Credit: Meshy (https://www.meshy.ai).

Changes made after generation: alpha made binary, stray specks removed, frames
moved vertically onto a shared ground line, and a second copy resized to half
size. Each frame's prompt and Meshy task id are in provenance.json beside its
master under art/masters/companion-frames/ and docs/design/live-room/painted/.
