The companion animation frames (blink, look-board, look-mat, nod, hop-crouch,
hop-air, think, cheer and talk for each companion) and the room's eighteen
unlock objects were generated with Meshy (https://www.meshy.ai) on its free
plan. Meshy licenses free-plan output under the Creative Commons Attribution
4.0 International license:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Credit: Meshy (https://www.meshy.ai).

The companion frames are image-to-image edits of each companion's painted idle
frame. Changes made after generation: alpha made binary, stray specks removed,
frames moved vertically onto a shared ground line, and a second copy resized to
half size. Each frame's prompt and Meshy task id are in provenance.json beside
its master under art/masters/companion-frames/ and
docs/design/live-room/painted/.

The unlock objects are image-to-image edits of a crop of the room each one
stands in, asking for that one object to be added. Changes made after
generation: the edit was resized to the crop's size, the object was cut out
where the edit differs from the crop, its alpha made binary, and it was
shrunk where needed to fit its slot's box. Each object's prompt, Meshy task id
and credits are in provenance.json beside its master under
art/masters/unlock-objects/.
